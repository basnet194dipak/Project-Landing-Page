Some random facts about space

The Sun

The sun is a star, not a planet. It makes up 99.86% of the total mass of our solar system.

Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system. Its more than 11 times wider than Earth.

Black Holes

Black holes have such strong gravity that even light cannot escape them.

Saturn

Saturn's rings are made of ice and rock, and they can be as thin as a few meters or as wide as hundreds of thousands of kilometers.

Nebula

Nebulas are the giant clouds of gas and dust where stars are born.
